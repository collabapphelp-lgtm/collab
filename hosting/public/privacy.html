Collab Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Summary

Collab connects musicians and content creators who want to work together. To do that, we collect what you put on your profile, the city you choose, who you like and pass on, and the messages you send to your connections.

We use this only to run Collab: to show you creatives who fit, connect you, and keep the community safe. We do not sell your information, and we do not show ads.

This policy applies to the Collab app for iPhone and any related website. Collab is operated by Kaamil Ajani ("Collab", "we", "us").

Everything below comes from you or from how you use the app. We do not buy data about you from anyone.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Create and run your account
  • Show your profile to other members and suggest creatives who fit what you're looking for
  • Create connections when two members like each other, and deliver your messages
  • Send notifications you've allowed, such as a new connection or message
  • Review reports, remove content or accounts that break our Terms of Service, and protect members
  • Fix problems and improve the app
  • Meet legal obligations

We do not sell or rent your personal information, share it with advertisers, or use it to show you ads. We do not use your messages or uploads to train AI models.

Who can see your information

  • Other signed-in members can see your profile: name, photo, city, creator type, skills, who you're looking for, genres or niches, showcase and bio. Your distance from them is shown, not your exact location.
  • Your connections can also message you and see the messages you send them. Only the two people in a conversation can read it.
  • No one else in the app can see your email address, your likes and passes, who you've blocked, or reports you've filed.
  • Our service providers (next section) store and process data for us, only to run Collab.
  • Legal reasons: we may share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety, our members, or Collab.
  • If Collab changes hands (for example, it's sold or becomes part of a company), your information may transfer with it under this policy. We'll tell you first.

What you post on your profile is visible to other members by design, so only share what you're comfortable showing.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account is open. You can change your profile at any time in Profile → Edit profile.

You can delete your account in the app at Profile → Delete account. Deleting it permanently removes:

  • Your profile, photo and uploaded files
  • Your likes, passes and blocks
  • Messages you sent (your conversations close for the other person)
  • Your login

Some data may remain for a limited time:

  • Copies in our providers' backups, until they expire on their normal schedule, which for Google Cloud is within about six months
  • Messages other members sent you, which belong to their side of the conversation
  • Reports and records we need to keep to protect members or meet legal obligations

You can also email collabapphelp@gmail.com to ask us to delete your account.

Your choices

  • Profile: edit or remove anything on your profile in the app.
  • Notifications: turn them off anytime in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Who you deal with: block or report anyone from their profile or your chat.
  • Access and deletion: email collabapphelp@gmail.com to ask for a copy of your information or to have it deleted. Depending on where you live, you may have additional legal rights; we'll honor requests the law gives you.

Children

Collab is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it. If you believe a minor is using Collab, contact collabapphelp@gmail.com.

Security

Your data is stored with Google Firebase and sent over encrypted connections. Access rules limit what each member can read: for example, only the two people in a conversation can read it. No system is perfectly secure, so use a password you don't use elsewhere.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
