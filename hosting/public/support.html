Help with the Collab app

Something not working, or have a question? Email collabapphelp@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you, usually within a day.

Getting started

  • Get the app: on iPhone, open collab-609ac.web.app/join. Android is coming soon; you can join the waitlist on the same page.
  • Set up your profile: pick your city, what you do, who you want to work with and your sound, then add at least one snippet or prompt.
  • Find people: browse Discover and tap ♥ on anyone you’d like to work with. When you both tap ♥, you’re connected and can message each other.
  • See who wants to collab: people who liked you show under “Wants to collab” on the Connections tab.

Your account

  • Edit your profile: Profile → Edit profile.
  • Forgot your password: on the login screen, tap “Forgot password?” and we’ll email you a reset link.
  • Notifications: turn them on or off in your iPhone’s Settings → Collab → Notifications.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, photos, uploads, likes and messages. You can also email us to ask.

Safety

  • Report or block someone: tap ••• on their profile or in your chat. Blocking closes the conversation and hides you from each other.
  • We review reports and aim to act within 24 hours. Offensive language is filtered, and accounts that break our Terms of Service are removed.
  • Meeting someone in person? Meet somewhere public and tell a friend where you’ll be.
